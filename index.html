From raw telecom, housing & social data to decisions a business can act on.

Six tasks across three real-world datasets — cleaned, modeled, and turned into a churn retention dashboard, a home-value estimator, and a social sentiment triage model. Built to show the full pipeline: data quality, modeling trade-offs, and the business actions each result actually supports.

The situation

This portfolio stands in for three analytics requests a data team gets asked to solve:

1. A telecom operator is losing 14.49% of its subscriber base and wants to know who is at risk and why — not just a churn score, but a reason a retention team can act on.

2. A real-estate desk wants a quick, explainable estimate of home value from structural and neighborhood features, without relying on a black box.

3. A social/brand team is drowning in unlabeled mentions and wants mentions auto-sorted into positive, negative, and neutral so human reviewers only touch what matters.

Each section below answers one of these with real metrics, real trade-offs, and a stated limitation — not just a chart.
